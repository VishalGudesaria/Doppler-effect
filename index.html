Understanding the Doppler Effect

"Why Does an Ambulance Sound Different When It Passes You?"

Jump to Simulation
🧍
🚑

Have you ever noticed...

...that an ambulance siren sounds different when it approaches you compared to when it moves away? When it comes toward you, the siren sounds high-pitched. The moment it passes and drives away, the pitch suddenly drops and sounds lower.

1. Approaching You

The sound waves get squished together.

  • Frequency goes UP
  • Wavelength gets SHORTER
  • Pitch sounds HIGHER

2. Moving Away

The sound waves stretch out.

  • Frequency goes DOWN
  • Wavelength gets LONGER
  • Pitch sounds LOWER
